## Risk behavior

There is **one** visible primary profile: `atenea-go`.

The handoff must state both:

```text
Cost policy: go
Risk class: volume | complex
```

For both risk classes, MiMo coordinates, Muse implements, Qwen performs Standards review and Luna High performs Spec review.

- `volume`: findings go first to a fresh Muse Go corrector.
- `complex`: findings go directly to a fresh DeepSeek V4.1 Flash Go corrector; require stronger deterministic closure and Cora integrated audit before merge recommendation when the work is material.
- The existing maximum of two fresh bounded correction attempts for the same already-authorized finding envelope remains unchanged. New material finding/scope change or blocker after attempt #2 => HUMAN STOP.

Do not add extra routine reviews merely because risk is `complex`. Risk hardening must remain proportional and deterministic-first.

## Cost / quota semantics

`go` is a human-selected cost policy, not a quota router.

Allowed normal providers/models are exactly the bindings above. In particular, the profile deliberately combines:

- OpenCode Go for coordinator/writer/merger/correction capacity;
- NaN `qwen3.6` only as the already no-counter explorer/Standards route;
- OpenAI Luna High for Spec assurance.

No other paid NaN model, OpenAI Sol model, Go model, or Free model is an automatic fallback.

If a bound model is unavailable, quota-blocked, removed or materially incapable during an active work unit: **STOP**. Do not silently switch providers/models. The human may choose another cost policy only at a clean boundary.

Do not implement percentage balancing, automatic 5-hour-window failover, quota polling as routing authority, or a model carousel.
