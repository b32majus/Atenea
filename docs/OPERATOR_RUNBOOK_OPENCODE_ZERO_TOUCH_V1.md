# Atenea — OpenCode zero-touch operator runbook v1

Status: **HISTORICAL QUALIFIED RUNBOOK — OpenCode 1.18.10**
Date: 2026-09-27

## 1. Human boundary before launch

The human accepts/shapes the bounded ticket or train and its publication boundary. Merge remains human-owned.

Before writer authority, prove mechanically where possible: exact repo/worktree/base, accepted issue/spec authority, clean tree, frozen principal oracle when applicable, allowed product scope and the current OpenCode runtime/model route.

Runtime preflight validates; it does not rediscover the whole product. Target ordinary preflight is seconds to a few minutes, not a second design phase.

## 2. Launch the thin supervisor

The supervisor owns only:

```text
authorized frontier
fresh process launch
exact already-authorized consent transport
checkpoint reconciliation
next ticket / STOP
```

It does not implement, review, choose reviewer verdicts or reproduce Gentle state.

## 3. Per ticket

Launch a **fresh OpenCode process**. Do not preserve train continuity through one giant model session.

The ticket process uses a direct bounded writer. Do not insert `gentle-orchestrator` as another ticket parent beneath the Atenea supervisor. The writer reads accepted authority, implements only the bounded scope and runs relevant deterministic verification.

Nontrivial writer-model routing remains C-071 qualification work. A route that shows prolonged design rumination before useful mutation is a profile failure, not a reason to restore persistent parent/compaction machinery.

## 4. Review / correction / burn

Gentle owns candidate identity, risk, review timing, reviewer tasks, correction and acknowledgement/burn.

When Gentle emits provider-owned `consent/v3`, the supervisor may relay only the exact candidate-scoped grant already covered by explicit train authority. It may not enlarge target/scope or manufacture a verdict. Standing session permission is not required by the normal path.

A fresh OpenCode lifecycle host may continue a durable Gentle lineage. On provider `collect`, execute the exact provider task; capture the exact result; follow provider-issued correction/re-review transitions; on APPROVED execute exact `acknowledge-approved`. Terminal consumption/burn is the close of that review authority.

## 5. Checkpoint and advance

After terminal review and project deterministic gates, reconcile exact HEAD, allowed paths and clean state, then create/preserve the durable checkpoint according to target-repo policy. Re-read the authorized frontier and launch a fresh OpenCode process for the next compatible ticket.

Do not require `/compact`, `/reload`, a long-lived model parent or a standing permission grant between tickets.

## 6. STOP conditions

STOP for genuine unresolved human authority/product meaning, contradictory accepted authority, irreversible/destructive risk, missing credentials/secret handling, or a runtime/provider failure that cannot be safely retried without changing authority.

Do not STOP merely because a ticket boundary occurred, context should be refreshed, a known exact consent envelope needs transport, or a deterministic checkpoint can be reconciled mechanically.

## 7. Human pre-merge boundary

After the authorized train is exhausted, run required composed-state/integration evidence and present the durable candidate for human audit/publication/merge decision. No automatic merge or force-push.
